SPEAKER-REVIEWED SLIDE ACCESS

Accessibility Ninja

Help blind and low-vision attendees follow the structure and meaning of conference slides.

Review a PowerPoint deck

Upload a small .pptx. We extract slide text and object order, flag items for review, and let the speaker approve descriptions in a navigable reading companion. Deck content is processed by this app; when Elasticsearch is configured, extracted text and approved explanations are also sent to that index.

Review results

Automated checks are prompts for human review, not an accessibility certification. Visual meaning and reading order need speaker review and assistive-technology testing.

Find an explanation

Prototype: supports PPTX text and image metadata checks. It does not rewrite the PowerPoint file, infer the right visual explanation, or establish that a deck is accessible.
